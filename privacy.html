Privacy Policy

Dragon Keeper for iPhone and iPad

HomeSupport

The short version

Dragon Keeper does not collect, store or share any information about you or your child. The game has no ads, no accounts, no tracking and no analytics.

What the game keeps

Your game progress is saved only on your own device. It never leaves the device, and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, the saved game is deleted too.

Internet

The game does not need the internet to play. It does not send data to us or to any other company.

Read aloud

The read-aloud button uses your device's own built-in voices. The game does not record or send any sound.

Children

Dragon Keeper is made for players of all ages, including children. Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
