Get help or report a problem

Open a new issue on our help page. Tell us what happened, what device you use, and the iOS version. A screenshot helps.

Common questions

  • There is no sound. Check that the device is not in silent mode. Then open ☰ in the game and check the 🔊 button.
  • Where is my saved game? The game saves on this device only. A saved game from the web version does not move into the app.
  • How do I move? Touch the screen and drag to fly. Tap 🔥 to breathe. A button shows when you can do something nearby.
  • Can my family play it? Yes. With Family Sharing, people in your family group can download it at no extra cost.
